# Skaldryne Contributor License Agreement — Draft for Legal Review

> **DRAFT — DO NOT ENABLE EXTERNAL CONTRIBUTIONS UNTIL THIS CLA HAS BEEN REVIEWED BY QUALIFIED COUNSEL.**

This file records the intended structure for the Skaldryne contributor agreement. It is deliberately not presented as a final legal instrument.

The intended CLA should provide that:

1. Contributors retain copyright ownership in their contributions.
2. Contributors represent that they have sufficient rights to submit their contributions.
3. Contributors grant the Skaldryne licensor a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright license sufficient to use, reproduce, modify, create derivative works, publicly display, publicly perform, distribute, and otherwise exploit their contributions.
4. The grant expressly includes the right to **sublicense and relicense contributions**, including under source-available, open-source, proprietary, and commercial licenses.
5. Appropriate patent rights are granted for patent claims necessarily infringed by the contribution, subject to attorney-approved defensive termination terms.
6. Contributors provide contributions without warranties except for representations expressly stated in the agreement.
7. A corporate CLA or equivalent authorization process should be used when an employer owns or may own contributed work.

## Why this matters

Skaldryne uses a dual-licensing strategy. The project therefore needs sufficient rights to distribute the combined codebase under the community license and under separately negotiated commercial licenses.
