## Dependency policy

New dependencies should be reviewed for compatibility with Skaldryne's source-available and commercial-licensing model.

Permissive licenses such as MIT, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, ISC, and Apache-2.0 are generally straightforward candidates, but every dependency and its actual terms should be reviewed.

Strong-copyleft, source-available, noncommercial, custom, or unclear licenses require explicit review before adoption.
