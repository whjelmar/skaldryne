# Skaldryne Trademark Policy — Initial Project Policy

The Skaldryne name, logos, and distinctive project branding are separate from the copyright license covering the software.

The software license does **not** grant permission to use Skaldryne trademarks in a way that implies sponsorship, endorsement, affiliation, or official status.

## Generally acceptable references

Subject to applicable trademark law, factual statements such as the following are generally intended to be acceptable:

- "Compatible with Skaldryne"
- "Plugin for Skaldryne"
- "Based on Skaldryne"

Such use should not imply that an unofficial fork, service, extension, or product is produced or endorsed by the Skaldryne project.

## Forks and derivative products

A fork or derivative product should use its own primary name and branding unless separate permission has been granted.

For example:

> Frostkeep — based on Skaldryne

rather than presenting an unofficial product as an official edition of Skaldryne.

## Logos

Do not use the Skaldryne logo as the primary logo of another product or service without written permission.
